Getting Started

Up & Running in 3 Steps

A seamless 60-second setup to bring your day to life with friendly creature companions.

01
👤

Personalize Your Experience

A 3-step onboarding sets up your personal Rouutine experience — fast and private.

  • Enter your first name — creatures greet and nudge you personally
  • Pick a Nudge Tone: 🌸 Gentle, ⚡ Energetic, or 🤣 Funny
  • Gentle is calm and warm. Energetic inspires action. Funny makes you smile
  • You can switch your tone anytime in Settings
  • Allow notification and exact alarm permissions for timely nudges
02
🐾

Build Your Daily Routines

Add routines for any part of your day — morning, afternoon, focus blocks, or evening wind-down.

  • Tap the + button on the Home screen
  • Give your routine a title (e.g. 'Morning Walk', 'Deep Focus', 'Wind Down')
  • Set a scheduled time and pick repeating days of the week
  • Choose a creature companion — each has a unique theme and default energy
  • Save — your routines arrange chronologically on the Home timeline
03
🔔

Get Nudged & Build Streaks

At the scheduled time, your creature sends a warm prompt. Complete or snooze — consistency counts.

  • A delightful notification arrives right on schedule
  • Tap to open the rich Moment screen with full creature art
  • Tap Done — confetti animates, sound chimes, and streak advances 🔥
  • Tap Snooze if you need a gentle reminder a few minutes later
  • Track your streaks and unlock milestone badges in the Progress tab

FAQ

Common Questions

Answers to everything you need to know about using Rouutine.

Does Rouutine need an internet connection? +
No — Rouutine is 100% offline. All routine schedules, streaks, companion artwork, and nudge messages work completely without internet. Notifications are scheduled directly on your phone using Android's local AlarmManager.
Will notifications arrive if my phone is restarted? +
Yes. Rouutine registers a boot receiver that automatically reschedules all your active routine alarms as soon as your phone finishes turning on. Please ensure notification and exact alarm permissions remain enabled in your Android system settings.
Can I change my companion creature or nudge tone later? +
Yes. You can switch your global nudge tone (🌸 Gentle, ⚡ Energetic, 🤣 Funny) anytime in Settings. You can also edit any routine by tapping its card on the Home screen to change its creature companion, scheduled time, or repeating days.
What happens if I miss a scheduled routine? +
If a scheduled routine is not completed before midnight, the streak for that routine pauses. You can complete it manually the following morning, but daily consistency is what powers streak increments. Our creatures never scold or nag you — they warmly cheer you on to start fresh.
How is my data stored and backed up? +
All your data is kept locally in an encrypted Room SQLite database on your device. In accordance with Google Play security compliance, device cloud backup rules protect your settings while keeping local database files strictly private to your phone.
Are there ads, subscriptions, or paywalls? +
Rouutine provides an ad-free, clutter-free experience. The core version supports up to 5 routine slots with creature companions, streak tracking, and full offline privacy. Any future pro tier will be an optional one-time purchase without recurring subscriptions.
