Section 03

Internet Access

Rouutine requires no internet connection. All routines, streaks, and creature nudge messages are stored locally. Notifications are scheduled using Android's AlarmManager — a local on-device mechanism. The app makes zero network requests.

Section 04

Notifications & Alarms

Rouutine uses Android's AlarmManager to schedule exact alarms for each routine. When an alarm fires:

  • A local notification is displayed on your device
  • No data is transmitted — the process is entirely local
  • Alarms are automatically rescheduled after device restart (BOOT_COMPLETED)
  • Revoking notification permission from Android Settings will prevent nudges from appearing
Section 05

A future premium version will include Family Sync. When this feature is released, this policy will be updated before it ships. The current offline-only version is and will remain unaffected.

Section 06

Data Deletion

To delete all data, simply uninstall the app. All routines, completion history, and preferences are permanently removed. No remote deletion is necessary.

Section 07
